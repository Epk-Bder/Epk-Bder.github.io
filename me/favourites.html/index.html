Lists of cools things I recommend

Music

  1. Ozma - Rock And Roll Part Three

    This album is quite niche, if you like Weezer PLEASE LISTEN TO IT ITS SO SO SO GOOD, Rivers Cuomo has evenly officially endorsed them, and with Rivers they wrote the song Eulogy for A Rock band which is on EWBAITE, My favourite track is either "Battlescars" or "If I Only Had A Heart" which are probably the 2 least Weezer-esque tracks on the album

  2. Weezer - Weezer (Blue Album)

    You can't resist her, she's in your bones I think the Base Version of this album is better than Pinkerton, my favourite track is "Only In Dreams"

  3. Masayoshi Takanaka - The Rainbow Goblins / Nijidensetsu

    Don't Even know what genre this is? Jazz Fusion Prog Rock?? Very nice listen, my favourite track is "You Can Never Come To This Place", Takanaka the goat

  4. Weezer - Pinkerton

    Super super good, My fav track is probably "Across the sea", if you include Deluxe Edition "Tragic Girl" and "You Gave Your Love To Me Softly" are definitely in contention

  5. Miles Davis - Get Up With It

    Most jarring sounding music you'll probably ever hear, I definitely do not listen regularly, but highly recommend a listen if you're into this stuff

I only have albums in here for now. So a lot of individual songs I like are not included, maybe they will be added another time

Video Games

  1. Final Fantasy VII

    Arguably the best game ever made, Materia is probably the best magic system in any RPG game, Amazing story aswell. Highly highly recommend, Remakes are good but definitely not as good

  2. Yo-Kai Watch

    Maybe the best 3DS JRPG (I haven't played SMT IV) Such an amazing game, I definitely don't believe its a copy of Pokemon, the amount of post game content and replayability in this game is genuinely absurd,

    Way more fun than any of the 3DS pokemon games

  3. Peggle Deluxe

    People treat this game as a bit of a joke, but it really is amazing, extremely fun gameplay like most popcap games,

    definitely worth buying on steam for like 99cent
